# First talk

## A couple of things before you fly…

### Speaker

[Alex Shelley](https://www.linkedin.com/in/adshelley/)

### Synopsis

From 1988 (Lockerbie) to 9/11 and Salisbury in 2018 – Airports and aviation
have often been the focus of Security.

There is a myriad of security controls in and around Airports or aviation
in general in addition to controls around humans and cargo traversing the
planet.

This talk will go through my life experience of securing them both,
including lessons learned during the assurance of them and as an seasoned
traveller thorough countries safe and precarious.
