# First talk

## The Great Recruitment Firewall

### Speaker

[Mariya Hristova](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mariya-hristova-assoc-cipd-95b63264/)

### Synopsis

Spies are lurking everywhere, but especially in the hiring pipeline. Disguised as the perfect candidate to try and snag a position in a company where they can espionage away!

Recruitment is the first point of contact for all candidates so in this
talk I will go over how I recognise fraudulent candidates without
descending into unfounded bias. I'll go over some recent examples and give
some practical guidance of what you can do if you are not sure that the
person opposite you is who they say they are.
